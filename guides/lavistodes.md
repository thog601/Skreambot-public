## Overview

A Custodes-based burst team heralded as a strong meta pick. The plan: enable
Kariyan to snowball, hold buffs from Trajann and Dante, position carefully, and
time your actives precisely for one big burst turn.

**Current comp**
- **Kariyan** (core damage / snowball)
- **Trajann** (buffer / synergy)
- **Dante** (buffer — ability damage)
- **Kharn** (damage dealer)
- **Laviscius** (utility / burst — *replaces Ragnar; this is the "Lavistodes" swap*)

> The turn-by-turn and tips below were written for the Ragnar version. Laviscius
> fills the same slot, but his exact kit and timing aren't documented here yet.

**When to run it:** Custodes get +25% damage vs Psykers & Daemons and take −40%
from Daemons / psychic damage — so it's especially strong on those bosses.

## Core Mechanics

- **Kariyan's active is the payoff:** using it *before* your other actives on the
  big turn maximises the stacking damage that follows.
- **Buffers are essential:** Trajann and Dante uplift ability damage, which feeds
  Kariyan, Kharn and the rest. Substituting either one weakens the team heavily.
- **Faction modifiers:** the +25% / −40% vs Psykers & Daemons is a real lever —
  pick those bosses when you can.

## Turn-by-Turn

**Turn 1**
- Move Kariyan toward the boss but avoid his basic attack if possible — you want
  him to survive and build momentum.
- Position Trajann and Dante to buff as many attackers as possible (ideally in
  range of Kariyan, Kharn and your utility slot).
- Cast buff actives early if it's safe, to raise your baseline for later turns.
- Use your damage units to soften primes depending on layout.
- Conserve Kariyan's active for later when the amplification matters more.

**Turn 2**
- Let Kariyan take normal attacks so stacks start building.
- Re-apply Dante / Trajann buffs when cooldowns allow.
- Weaken primes / reposition so more of the team can reach the boss next turn.

**Turn 3 (mid game)**
- Kariyan should have stacks or conditions met by now.
- Push damage aggressively with your damage units.
- Rebuff via Dante / Trajann to rebuild anything dropped or answer boss
  resistances.

**Final / big burst turn**
- Use **Kariyan's active first**, before your other actives — it amplifies the
  rest of that turn's attacks.
- Immediately follow with Trajann / Dante buffs if off cooldown.
- Pour everything into the boss.
- Clear lingering minions/primes first if they'd block buffs or AoE.

## Tips & Edge Cases

- **Kariyan HP timing:** aim to have him below ~50% HP by round 5–6 with his
  active ready — that sets up the big hit.
- **Keep buffs alive:** track buff durations and reapply as soon as possible, or
  your damage collapses.
- **Survive to the burst:** use Trajann / Dante to soak or suppress early damage
  so Kariyan lives to the late-game payoff.
- **Boss movement:** some bosses jump / reposition — anticipate it to keep
  Kariyan in his best lane.
- **Faction targeting:** lean into Psyker / Daemon bosses for the damage bonus.

## Summary

Abstracted sequence:
- **T1:** cast buffs → move Kariyan into range → chip with your damage units →
  conserve big actives.
- **T2:** keep buffing / positioning → start layering Kariyan attacks.
- **T3:** build pressure → get Kariyan below 50% HP if the burst needs it.
- **Final turn:** Kariyan active → immediately other buffs → everyone attacks →
  maximise hits.
