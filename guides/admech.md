## Overview

One of the highest-damage setups for Guild Raids. The whole plan is to maximise
Rho's damage by keeping him next to the boss, using Vitruvius's targeting bonus
to hand everyone extra shots, and spamming Tan's summons to fuel Actus's passive
and active shots.

**Core comp**
- **Rho** — primary damage dealer
- **Vitruvius** — target marker; grants extra shots
- **Tan** — summoner + burst switch
- **Actus** — summon enabler + active chain shots
- **Sho** (secondary) — extra summons + support fire

**When to run it:** as a top-tier GR damage team, and especially strong on
bosses that land multiple hits (e.g. Gulgotz) — each of the boss's hits procs
Rho.

## Core Mechanics

- Rho's passive scales if he *starts a turn* next to the boss — position him
  early and keep him there.
- Vitruvius's targeting buff gives +1 shot to all allies who hit the same
  target. It stays until Vitruvius attacks a different target or dies.
- Actus's heal helps Tan create summons; his active makes all nearby allies
  shoot — use it when 3+ shooters can hit the boss for a big burst.
- Tan's summons are critical: they fuel Rho's passive, and they can be procced
  to fire when Tan switches to his "hitty" (Conqueror) mode.
- Summon cap = 5. Managing this is key to sustaining high damage.

## Turn-by-Turn

**Turn 1**
1. Move Rho next to the boss → don't attack yet. His proximity buff matters more.
2. Move Vitruvius forward and shoot the boss → sets up the +1 shot buff for
   everyone.
3. Activate Rho's ability → he now hits harder this turn and next.
4. Move Tan into range → don't attack yet.
5. Position Actus near Tan.
6. Shoot with Tan, then use Actus's heal on Tan → summons two Tan servitors
   immediately.
7. Summon Sho's drone.

End of Turn 1: Rho is adjacent to the boss, Vitruvius's buff is active, and you
have 2 Tan summons + 1 Sho summon.

**Turn 2**
- Keep Rho adjacent for maximum damage.
- Shoot the boss with everyone, including summons.
- If 3+ shooters are in range, pop Actus's active to chain shots.
- Start working toward 4 Tan summons by shooting again with Tan.

**Turn 3**
- You should have 4 summons by now (max is 5). To cap them:
  1. Move Tan into position.
  2. Move Actus so he can target Tan again.
  3. Use Actus's ability on Tan *before* Tan shoots → creates your 5th summon.
- Switch Tan into Conqueror ("hitty") mode → when Tan attacks, all adjacent
  summons also shoot. Trigger this proc window before firing Tan to maximise
  summon DPS.

**Peak burst:** using Rho's active and Actus's active while Tan has 5 summons
and is in Conqueror mode is your maximum-devastation turn.

## Tips & Edge Cases

- **Summon management:** if summons die, switch Tan back to summoning mode and
  rebuild. Always keep 4–5 up to get full value from Actus's active and Tan's
  proc synergy.
- **Vitruvius buff:** it adds +1 hit for all attacks against his *last* target
  and persists until he shoots a different one — so keep targeting the boss to
  hold sustained fire without wasting his action.
- **Advanced Actus timing:** always look for a window where 3+ shooters can hit
  the boss (Tan summons, Sho summons, Big Blue if present), then trigger his
  active after repositioning summons to maximise value.
- **Positioning:** summons act before you move Rho. Against bosses that move
  predictably (e.g. Dorn), move Rho *last* — place him where the boss will end
  up so he's adjacent for the next turn's big swing.

## Summary

| Goal | How |
| --- | --- |
| Keep Rho next to boss | Move him first on Turn 1, never let him drift |
| Maintain Vitruvius +1 buff | Always target the boss first, avoid mis-targeting |
| Maximise summons | Build to 5 quickly, rebuild if any die |
| Chain Actus's active | Pop it when 3+ shooters are next to him |
| Switch Tan's mode | At 5 summons, change to Conqueror mode |
| Reposition Rho last | Anticipate boss movement for constant adjacency |

**TL;DR** — the comp stacks multiplicative synergies: Rho adjacent → huge base
damage; Vitruvius → +1 shot per hit; Tan + Sho summons → free extra shooters;
Actus's active → chain reaction; Tan's Conqueror mode → summons fire again. Run
it smoothly and your GR damage skyrockets.
